# User guide

1. Create an account and set your professional goals, practice duration, and self-assessed level. The self-assessment shapes explanations; it is not verified.
2. Optionally submit a short workplace writing sample. The baseline reports only a limited set of detected text conventions and does not assess speaking or vocabulary from prose.
3. Open the practice plan, submit a short written answer, and review any tracked issues. The dashboard and progress page reflect saved activity; the free-text checker does not grade overall English proficiency.
4. Use the writing coach to explore grammar, clarity, concision, professional tone, confidence, or leadership modes. Optional model suggestions preserve your facts as instructed, but review them before use. Keep confidential information out of practice text.
5. Rate vocabulary recall to schedule the next review. Spaced intervals depend on your response.
6. In Settings, update preferences, export your stored data, or delete your account and records.

The current application is text-first. Workplace conversations are fictional qualitative practice; microphone capture, transcription, and acoustic evaluation are not implemented. Practice records are private to the learner and are not a job-performance rating.
